Panel mockups

Rough sketches on made-up data, not pixel-exact and not code. They show structure and what is removed, so you can pick by feel. Keys stay visible (DESIGN.md principle 4), the Focused Loop stays first (principle 2).

Today: status line, big title, "last worked on", an action row, the Where I left off box, "last seen", Anchors, then search, chip, "Add as Anchor" and a footer. Four places hold keys and the content scrolls inside the panel.

A: One footer for all keys

All keys move to one footer line. Content is only title, Where I left off and Anchors. "Last worked on" and "Last seen" are gone. The search sits in a single quiet row.
