unfocused doesn't replace your todo app, your notes or your team's tickets. The work stays where it is. unfocused keeps a list of what you started, links each entry to the exact spot, and shows the list whenever you switch.

One key from any app

⌃Space opens the panel over whatever is in front. Everything you started is there, Loose Ends first. ↩ Focuses a Loop and opens its Anchors, such as the Slack thread, the draft or the page you were on.

The Focused Loop comes first

While you're Focused, the panel is about that one Loop. It shows where you left off, who you're waiting on, and Close Loop as the first action. A new Loop takes ⌘↩, one key more than finishing.

Where you left off

When you switch to another Loop, unfocused asks where you left off before the other app takes the screen. You write one line. Next time you Focus that Loop, the line is the first thing you see.

What it keeps for you

Anchors back to the work

Each Loop links to the exact spot where the work is: the Slack conversation, the Mail message, the page in Safari, Chrome or Firefox, the note in Obsidian, the file in Pages. ⌘O opens all of them.

Loose Ends come back

A Loop nobody touched for three active days becomes a Loose End. Weekends and holidays don't count, only days you used your Mac. The Tie-off shows the Loose Ends one at a time, and for each you choose Focus, Close Loop, Let go or Not now.

Where your time went

unfocused also records which window was in front and counts the time toward the Loop it belongs to. The Recap shows what you finished, what moved forward and how much time went elsewhere. You see what pulled you away, and you have a record of what you got done. Copy it as Markdown for your standup.

Private by design

The way you get things done is personal. unfocused never shares your Activity, not with a server, not with an advertiser, not with anyone you work with. It notes which window was in front, never what's in it or what you type, and it skips 1Password and private windows.

Why I made this

I start things everywhere. A draft in Notion, a Slack thread I promised to answer, a review someone asked me for. Then something urgent comes up and I switch. By Friday I've started more than I've finished, and I can't say where the week went.

None of my tools sees all of it. My todo app has the tasks I planned, the tickets have the team's work, my notes have my notes. None of them knows what I started.

unfocused doesn't replace any of them. It remembers what I started, and when I'm about to start something else, it shows me what's already open.

It also shows me where my time went. I notice what pulls me away, and I can see the work I got done.

It runs on my Mac and nowhere else. There's no account and no server, and nothing is shared with anyone.

Questions

What does it cost? Nothing. unfocused is free, and its source is on GitHub.

What do I need? macOS 26 or later. The written Recap summary and the check whether a window belongs to your Focused Loop use Apple Intelligence; without it, unfocused falls back to plain rules and everything else works.

Which apps can it anchor to? Pages in Safari, Chrome and Firefox, Slack conversations, Mail messages, Notion pages, Obsidian notes, Todoist tasks, files in Finder, and documents open in Pages, Numbers, Preview or TextEdit. Any other app gets an Anchor that reopens it with its window title.

Does it read what I type? No. Activity records which app and window was in front, and when. It never records content, keystrokes or screenshots.

Why does it ask for Accessibility? To read window titles, so Capture can grab the exact thread or document in front of you. It asks only when a feature first needs it, and Capture works without it.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it? The builds aren't notarized by Apple yet. Approve it once in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway, after installing and after each update.

How do I update? Run brew upgrade --cask unfocused. Your Loops and the Accessibility permission stay.

Where is my data? In ~/Library/Application Support/unfocused/, one SQLite file. Logs are in ~/Library/Logs/unfocused/.

How do I remove it? Run brew uninstall --cask unfocused. Delete the folder above to remove your data as well.

Is it a todo app? No. It doesn't replace your todo app, your notes or your tickets, and it has no due dates, projects or priorities. Your work stays where it is. unfocused remembers that you started it.

Can my employer see it? No. unfocused is 100% local and never shares your Activity, not with a server, an advertiser or anyone you work with. There's no account and no sync.

Will it nag me? No. The only notification it sends is an optional daily Tie-off reminder, at a time you choose.

Install

With Homebrew, in Terminal:

brew tap stefanhoth/unfocused https://github.com/stefanhoth/unfocused
brew install --cask stefanhoth/unfocused/unfocused

Then open unfocused from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app, because the builds aren't notarized yet. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You do this once after installing and once after each update.
